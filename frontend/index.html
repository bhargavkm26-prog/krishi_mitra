The Governance Dilemma

Agrarian Welfare is Fragmented

Despite over 2,000 public assistance schemes, millions of Indian farmers are excluded by procedural bureaucracy, opaque eligibility criteria, and disjointed administrative portals.

2,066 Disconnected Portals

Schemes exist across 28 separate state revenue boards and dozens of central ministries. Farmers have no unified interface to cross-reference overlapping benefits, input quotas, and subsidy thresholds.

40%+ Rejection from Minor Errors

Spelling mismatches in Aadhaar, unseeded bank accounts on the NPCI mapper, and unverified mutation entries stop PM-KISAN, PMFBY, and solar subsidies without clear procedural redressal.

Cloud Vulnerability & Latency

Commercial cloud LLMs expose sensitive farmer identities, landholding coordinates, and family income data to third-party offshore APIs while suffering from severe latency and hallucinated policy advice.
